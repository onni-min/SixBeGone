SixBeGone settings

SixBeGone needs access to YouTube to remove GTA 6 content there.

Extra keywords

One per line. Any post containing one of these is removed, on top of the built-in GTA 6 list.

Always show

One per line. Posts containing one of these phrases are never removed.

Blocked channels

One per line: a channel handle (like @example) or channel ID. Everything from them is removed.
